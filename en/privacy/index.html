02Principles of processing

As a rule, we process our users' personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and our content and services. We process personal data only where there is a legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR. Which one applies is stated in each section.

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (e.g. name, address, email address, IP address).

Possible legal bases are in particular: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (point b), legal obligation (point c) and legitimate interest (point f).

03Hosting & server log files

This website is provided as a static site via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (part of Microsoft).

When you visit the website, GitHub as the host automatically collects information that your browser transmits and stores it in so-called server log files. This may include in particular:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested and amount of data transferred
  • referrer URL (previously visited page)
  • browser type, language and operating system

This data serves the technical provision, security and stability of the website and is not combined with other data sources.

As GitHub may process data in the USA, data is transferred to a third country. GitHub bases this on appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU standard contractual clauses / EU-US Data Privacy Framework). For more information, see GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, efficient provision)

05Audience measurement (own count, without cookies)

To understand which content is in demand, we count page views with our own, very data-minimising measurement. No cookies are set, no identifiers are assigned and no IP addresses are stored. Recognising individual people is therefore technically impossible.

Only three items are stored per view: the page visited (e.g. "/preise/"), the name of the referring website (e.g. "instagram.com" – never the full address or search terms) and the rough device class ("mobile" or "desktop"). All other information is discarded on receipt.

In the request form we count in the same way which of the five steps was shown and whether the request was sent – without your entries and without an identifier. This shows us where the form is too complicated.

The evaluation takes place on our own infrastructure (Cloudflare D1, location EU); there is no disclosure to third parties and no combination with other data sets. If your browser signals "Do Not Track", nothing is counted at all.

As no personal data is processed and there is no access to your device within the meaning of § 25 TDDDG, neither consent nor a cookie banner is required.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in designing the website according to demand)

08Free preview

On start.social2scale.com you can have a free preview of your Instagram presence built. For this we process your email address, your name or Instagram name, your details on your work and topic and, if you upload one, your profile photo. We first send you a confirmation email; the preview is only created after you confirm. We delete unconfirmed requests after 30 days.

The post texts are written from your details by an AI language model from Anthropic (Claude). You can request deletion at any time (No. 16). If you voluntarily allow it, we show your preview without your name as an example; you can withdraw this consent at any time by messaging info@social2scale.com.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (creating the requested preview); Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (permission to show as an example); Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (abuse prevention by checking the photo)

14Community (Skool)

For exchange with us and among members, we run a community on the Skool platform of Skool.com, Inc., USA. Members are clients of social2scale and prospects who are just getting to know us. Taking part is voluntary. Your contractual services do not depend on it.

Who is responsible?

We are responsible for the group: for its settings, admitting and removing members, the joining questions and recordings. According to its own statements, Skool processes the group's content on our behalf. Skool does not publicly offer a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR; we have requested one. For your Skool account itself (sign-up, profile, use of other groups), Skool is responsible under its own terms.

Which data?

Name, profile picture and profile information, posts, comments and reactions, your activity in the group, participation in live calls and your answers to the joining questions (consent, Instagram name). If you consent via your Social Hub, we also store your decision there: time, your answers and the version of the text you saw.

Who sees what?

All members see your name, profile picture, posts and activity. This may reveal that you are in contact with us. As operator, we also see your answers to the joining questions. We use the Instagram name to match members to our client directory (No. 13).

Transfer to the USA

Skool processes the data in the USA. Skool is not certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (official list checked on 1 November 2026), and no EU standard contractual clauses have been agreed between us and Skool. We therefore base the transfer on your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). The risk: in the USA your data is not protected to an equivalent level. US authorities can access it without you having the same legal remedies as in the EU.

Live calls

We only record a live call if we announce it in advance, and only people who have given separate consent. Anyone who has not consented takes part without camera and microphone during the recording, or we pause the recording. We make recordings available to all members in the group on Skool. We keep a copy ourselves (Cloudflare R2, No. 13, and a backup on our own computers), where we also create the subtitles. We delete all copies, including the one on Skool, no later than 12 months after the call. After a withdrawal, we cut you out of existing recordings; where that is not fully possible, we delete the recording.

How long?

We keep your decisions about the community (consent, change, exit) as evidence until the end of the 3rd year after you leave. We delete your answers to the joining questions and the match to our client directory when you leave. When you leave, we also delete your posts and comments in the group; if you want them to stay, tell us when you leave. Your membership lasts until you leave or we close the group. You delete your Skool account yourself at Skool.

Withdrawal and leaving

You can leave at any time and withdraw any consent with effect for the future: in the Social Hub using the leave or change button, or with an informal message to info@social2scale.com. We remove you from the group without undue delay. Prospects without a Social Hub send a message to Phil in Skool or to info@social2scale.com.

More information: Skool privacy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (membership); Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR (transfer to the USA); separate consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 22 KUG (recordings); Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (evidence of consent, Art. 7(1) GDPR)

15Storage period

We process and store personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention periods (e.g. commercial and tax retention periods of up to 10 years).

Server log files are regularly deleted or anonymised by the host after a short time. We delete request data from the form as soon as it is no longer needed to handle your request or perform the contract and no statutory retention obligations apply.

16Your rights

As a data subject, you have in particular the following rights under the GDPR:

  • right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • right to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

To exercise your rights and for any questions about data protection, an informal message to info@social2scale.com is sufficient.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. An informal message to info@social2scale.com is sufficient.

17Right to lodge a complaint

Without prejudice to any other remedy, under Art. 77 GDPR you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you consider that the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR.

The competent authority is usually the supervisory authority of your habitual residence or that of the controller. For the controller this is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach (www.lda.bayern.de).
