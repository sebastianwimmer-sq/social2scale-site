Process

Four phases. From month two, you do nothing.

From the first conversation to a presence that keeps running without you. Here’s what happens in each phase, what you have afterwards, and how much of your time it takes.

01

Intro call & strategy

We map out your system before anything gets built. Goal, audience, where you stand today. And whether it’s a fit at all. If it isn’t, we’ll tell you.

What you have afterwards

A plan that fits your business. Not just your industry.

Your effort

Two calls. First getting to know Phil, then the strategy call.

02

Setup & onboarding

Your account gets a face: profile, branding and content strategy. We build it as if it had always been there.

What you have afterwards

A profile that someone seeing you for the first time takes seriously.

Your effort

One briefing, about 30 minutes.

03

Content & management

Ongoing production, week after week. Idea, copy, design, publishing. The work that otherwise gets left lying between your appointments.

What you have afterwards

Posts that go live without you having to think about them.

Your effort

None. You only approve posts if you want to.

04

Scaling

Reach turns into inquiries. Whatever works gets more push.

What you have afterwards

Numbers in your client area instead of gut feeling.

Your effort

None.

What it costs you in time

Your time, in proportion.

  1. If you do it yourself7.5–15 hours a month
  2. With us: month 1about 30 minutes, once
  3. With us: from month 2nothing

Based on ten posts a month at 45 to 90 minutes per post. Idea, copy, design, publishing. The same math, with numbers, is on the pricing page.

What we need from you.

Access to your account

Your existing profile, or we create one. The rights stay with you. Always.

Images

What you already have is usually enough. If not, we arrange a photo shoot.

Half an hour

The briefing in the first month. After that, only if you want to weigh in.

Let’s talk about your growth.

First we take a quick look at whether we’re a good fit. Then a preview or a call.
