Week 48 · November 25, 2026 · 1 min · new every Wednesday

Week 48 on social media.

InstagramYou can now fix a typo in your story for up to 15 minutes after posting.

Pick the edit option in the story's More menu; views, likes and replies stay, and the story is marked as edited. It's rolling out gradually, reports don't mention Germany specifically, and it's unclear whether you can swap the photo or video itself.

Source: Engadget, November 18, 2026

YouTubeYou can cut vertical Shorts from your long videos right inside YouTube Studio.

Pick a section by timestamp or transcript; captions are added where available. It's aimed mainly at podcasters, eligible channels and languages aren't confirmed, and AI suggestions are limited. Private, unlisted or made-for-kids Shorts won't link to the full video.

Source: Search Engine Journal, November 12, 2026

FacebookMeta's updated Creator Dashboard now runs in your computer's browser too.

You get live previews, bulk video uploads and a way to invite people who saw your content to follow you. It's for creator profiles, while businesses have the Business Dashboard. Whether every account in Germany has access yet isn't confirmed.

Source: Social Media Today, November 18, 2026

PsychologyKeep captions on for your Shorts and proofread them before you publish.

A review of more than 100 studies found that captions help people understand a video, pay attention and remember more. People who are hard of hearing or still learning the language benefit most.

Source: Gernsbacher, M. A. (2015). Video Captions Benefit Everyone. Policy Insights from the Behavioral and Brain Sciences, 2(1), 195-202
